# GL.iNet Account

**Note**: The content on this page is based on firmware v4.10.

---

On the left side of the web Admin Panel, go to **CLOUD SERVICES** -> **GL.iNet Account**.

The GL.iNet Account allows you to connect and manage your devices and cloud services. With a single GL.iNet Account, you can seamlessly access GoodCloud and the GL.iNet App, providing a more convenient way to manage your network.

In addition, if you log in to your GL.iNet Account on this page, GoodCloud will enable Remote GUI and Remote SSH by default. You can also use GoodPAS via GoodCloud to quickly establish a secure connection between your travel router and home network, enabling seamless remote access when you are away from home.

## Enable GL.iNet Account

To bind your GL.iNet Account, please follow the steps below.

1. click **Get Started**. A URL will then be displayed on the page. You can click the **link** to log in via the GL.iNet Account page, or copy it for remote binding.

    ![account](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/account.png){class="glboxshadow"}

    ![bind account](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/bind_account.png){class="glboxshadow"}

2. Log in with your GL.iNet account. If you don’t have an account, sign up for one and log in. 

    ![login goodcloud](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/loin_goodcloud.png){class="glboxshadow" width=350}

3. Once logged in, the page displays the **Bound Account** and **Bound Time**. You can manage the binding between the router and your GL.iNet account on this page. But GoodPAS is disabled by default. To enable this feature, please navigate to the GoodPAS page and configure it.

    ![account 1](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/account_1.png){class="glboxshadow"}
    
    On this page, you can simply check the Enabled/Disabled label in the lower-left corner of each service icon to see its current status.

## Services

You can choose the appropriate entry to access the function page according to your needs.

### GoodCloud

Click **Go** in the lower-right corner of the GoodCloud icon to open the GoodCloud platform, where you can manage your device remotely. For details, please refer to [GoodCloud](./cloud.md).

![account 2](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/account_2.png){class="glboxshadow"}
    
![goodcloud](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/goodcloud.png){class="glboxshadow"}

### GoodPAS

Click **Go** in the lower-right corner of the GoodPAS icon to access the GoodPAS page, where you can configure it to enable seamless remote access to your home network. For specific configuration details, please refer to [GoodPAS](./astrowarp.md).

![goodpas 1](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/goodpas.png){class="glboxshadow"}

![goodpas 2](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/goodpas_page.png){class="glboxshadow"}

### glinet App

Click **GO** in the lower-right corner of the GL.iNet icon to go to the app download center. You can download the glinet App, which allows you to manage router easily from your phone.

![glinet](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/glinet.png){class="glboxshadow"}

![glinet download](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/glinet_app_download.png){class="glboxshadow" width=500}

## Unbind GL.iNet Account

If you want to unbind this device from your GL.iNet Account, click **Unbind**. Your device will then be unbound from your GL.iNet Account.

![unbind device](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/unbind_account.png){class="glboxshadow"}

Alternatively, if you manage your device via GoodCloud, go to **Devices** -> **Bound Devices**. Select the device, click **Settings** in the upper right corner, and choose **Delete**. The device will then be unbound from your GL.iNet Account.

![unbind goodcloud](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/ubbind_goodcloud.png){class="glboxshadow"}
