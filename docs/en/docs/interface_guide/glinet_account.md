# GL.iNet Account

**Note**: The content on this page is based on firmware v4.10.

---

On the left side of the web Admin Panel, go to **CLOUD SERVICES** -> **GL.iNet Account**.

A GL.iNet Account allows you to connect and manage your devices and cloud services. With a single GL.iNet Account, you can seamlessly access GoodCloud and the GL.iNet App for more convenient network management.

In addition, you can use GoodPAS to quickly establish a secure connection between your travel router and home network, enabling seamless remote access when you are away from home.

## Enable GL.iNet Account

To bind your GL.iNet Account, please follow the steps below.

1. Click **Get Started**.

    ![account](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/account.png){class="glboxshadow"}

    A URL will then be displayed on the page. Click the link and you will be directed to the GL.iNet Account login page. You can also copy it for remote binding.

    ![bind account](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/bind_account.png){class="glboxshadow"}

2. Log in with your GL.iNet account. If you don't have an account, sign up for one and log in.

    ![login goodcloud](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/loin_goodcloud.png){class="glboxshadow" width=350}

3. After logging in, your device will be bound to your account, and the page displays the **Bound Account** and **Bound Time**.

    ![account 1](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/account_1.png){class="glboxshadow"}

    On this page, you can simply check the enabled status of each service. The GoodCloud and GL.iNet App services are enabled by default, while the GoodPAS is disabled. To enable GoodPAS, please navigate to the GoodPAS page and configure it.

## Services

You can choose the appropriate services to manage your device according to your needs.

### GoodCloud

Click **Go** in the lower-right corner of the GoodCloud section to open the GoodCloud platform, where you can manage your device remotely. For details, please refer to [GoodCloud](./cloud.md).

![account 2](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/account_2.png){class="glboxshadow"}

### GoodPAS

Click **Go** in the lower-right corner of the GoodPAS section to access the GoodPAS page, where you can establish a secure network between your travel router and your home network. This enables seamless remote access to your home network, and you can use your home IP for Internet access. For details, please refer to [GoodPAS](./astrowarp.md).

![goodpas 1](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/goodpas.png){class="glboxshadow"}

![goodpas 2](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/goodpas_page.png){class="glboxshadow"}

### glinet App

Click **GO** in the lower-right corner of the GL.iNet section to go to the app download center. You can download the glinet App, which allows you to manage your router easily on your phone.

![glinet](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/glinet.png){class="glboxshadow"}

![glinet download](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/glinet_app_download.png){class="glboxshadow" width=500}

## Unbind GL.iNet Account

If you want to unbind this device from your GL.iNet Account, click **Unbind**. The device will then be unbound.

![unbind device](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/unbind_account.png){class="glboxshadow"}

Alternatively, if you manage your device via GoodCloud, go to **Devices** -> **Bound Devices**. Select the device, click **Settings** in the upper right corner, and choose **Delete**. The device will then be unbound from your GL.iNet Account.

![unbind goodcloud](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/router/en/4/interface_guide/glinet_account/ubbind_goodcloud.png){class="glboxshadow"}
